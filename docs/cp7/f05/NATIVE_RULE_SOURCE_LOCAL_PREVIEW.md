# Current rule conditions, shared episodes and local rehearsal

This candidate adds a typed, explicit read surface for current production,
accessory, Native sales AR and Native material AP conditions. The exact saved
analysis remains immutable. Financial conditions reuse the existing authorized
Native readers and due dates; there is no second balance engine. Outstanding
with no recorded due date remains data review. A proven zero balance can resolve
the business condition even when its due date is unknown.

Production and accessory values are the exact Original facts. An assumed zero
means the selected scenario has no alert, not physical resolution. A changed
Original becomes source review. Issue80 is not installation80; unproved physical
installation and eligible supply remain UNKNOWN. Opening AR/AP, payroll,
accessory AP and laundry AP remain explicitly NOT_COMPOSED. This bounded
candidate cannot claim complete F04/F05 acceptance.

All conditions and selected whole-version policies are bound to one source
hash. Time-dependent eligibility is displayed separately and rechecked when
claiming and finishing. Read source coverage is complete within the actor's
current rights. Financial rows and saved financial observations are never
exposed after current domain permission loss.

An explicit saved observation opens or updates a shared business episode.
Repeated observations preserve its identity and first-observed age. Unknown
evidence preserves an active episode with UNKNOWN freshness. ACK/DONE and
local outcomes cannot close it. Actual lawful settlement closes the episode;
its Native inverse opens a new episode linked to the immutable predecessor.
An absent or excluded domain is never a zero or implicit resolution.

The local binding is actor-owned, append-only, explicitly enabled and limited
to LOCAL_TEST_SINK. There is no provider, recipient, transport or scheduler.
A claim pins the reviewed binding, environment, source, episode, policy,
occurrence and fence. Concurrent distinct UUIDs for one occurrence yield one
claim. Its human-readable UTF-8 body is byte-hashed and checked by the receiver.
Finish uses the same binding/episode lock order and rechecks current rights,
source, rule, quiet period, cooldown and active episode after waits.

The local outcomes are CLAIMED, LOCAL_SINK_CAPTURED, SUPPRESSED and UNKNOWN.
None means SENT. UNKNOWN prevents automatic resend, including new UUIDs and
bindings. An explicit manual resolution records CAPTURE_CONFIRMED or
NOT_CAPTURED_CONFIRMED without changing the old claim/body/fence. A confirmed
non-capture permits one new manual attempt with a new fence; a capture counts
for current cooldown. Resolution does not close the business episode.

Every operation has exact actor/UUID payload replay and sealed absent lookup.
The browser stores command intent only. After a lost reply/remount it uses the
same UUID and payload. Reads and current403 retire every source-derived fact,
local body and parent FG/HPP while preserving unsent operator input. No RPC runs
on mount or tab change. ERP tab locks and sibling pending-intent checks remain.

Policy history uses stable25-row descending pages with a pinned through-version.
Appending a new version cannot displace the current history traversal. Target
history requires the same current Original scope and rights.
