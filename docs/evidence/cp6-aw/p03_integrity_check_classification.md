## 2. Conventions used in the table

- `v_end := (p_through+1)::timestamp AT TIME ZONE 'Asia/Jakarta'`, the same value the engine uses. `BD(ts) := erp._cp3_business_date(ts)`, so `ts < v_end` means `BD(ts) <= p_through`. `JD(j) := least(j.economic_date, j.transaction_date)`.
- **Anchor**: the earliest business date among all the business dates the offending row or entity carries. A mis-dated journal lands on a different date from its document, so the anchor always takes `least(...)` over the document date and the journal `economic_date`/`transaction_date`. `account_daily_balances` is keyed by `transaction_date`, while reports and `PO_JOURNAL` use `economic_date`.
- **Fail closed**: a DATABLE row whose anchor evaluates to NULL must block every date (CURRENT_STATE).
- **Model**: the check is evaluated on the current corrected state, including current statuses and current reversals, and then restricted to rows whose anchor is ≤ `p_through`. This is the same "facts dated ≤ D, reversed pairs excluded" model the engine's detectors use. Restricting by anchor is **monotone** by construction: once a row blocks date X, it blocks every date after X.
- In the basis column, **E** marks an exact as-of restriction (cumulative facts ≤ D) and **C** marks a conservative anchor (the entity's first business date, which over-blocks but never under-blocks).
- Entity anchors that need a helper function (not yet written):
  - `PO_ANCHOR(po)`: least of `BD()` over every fact carrying the PO id: `cutting_groups.cut_at`; `material_stock_movements.physical_at` for CUTTING_GROUP and CUTTING_GROUP_RETURN; `contractor_material_issues.physical_at`; `work_completion_events.physical_at`; `sewing_terminal_events.physical_at`; `wip_stage_events.physical_at`; `laundry_deliveries.physical_at`; `bs_cases.physical_at`; `fg_lots.produced_at`; `JD` of journals with `journal_lines.po_id`; import-source opening dates; attendance-pool and cost-adjustment dates. The engine's RECOST lateral covers only 4 of these fact kinds plus `recalc_from`, so it is **not** a safe PO anchor for the WIP/HPP book checks.
  - `PRODUCT_ANCHOR(p)`: least of `BD(fg_lots.produced_at)` of the product's non-PO lots, `JD` of journals with `journal_lines.product_id = p`, and the dates of the product's sales and returns.
  - `CUSTOMER_ANCHOR(c)`: least of `BD(sales_headers.sale_date)`, `BD(sales_payments.payment_date)`, the `opening_date` of the customer's openings, and `JD` of AR lines with `customer_id = c`.
  - `PURCHASE_ANCHOR(h)`: least of `BD(h.physical_at)`, `BD(supplier_payments.payment_date)`, the invoice dates of its posted invoice lines, `BD(material_supplier_returns.physical_at)` of its returns, and `JD` of journals with `source_id = h.id`.
- Runner codes:
  - INT = run_integrity_checks.
  - V267 = run_v267 direct. A `+V268` suffix means v268 re-emits it through pre_scope.
  - V267/ipc, V267/ppc and V267/pfc = through `initial_prepayment_checks_v1`, `pocket_period_checks_v1` and `pocket_fabric_checks_v1`.
  - V268/pre = run_v268 through pre_scope.
  - V268 = run_v268 direct.

## 5. UNCERTAIN list

1. **V2620T_JOURNAL_FUTURE_BUSINESS_DATE.** The journal's own columns give only an upper bound for the correct date: correct date ≤ `BD(posting_at)` < `economic_date`. The true lower bound is the source document's business date. The check reaches that document only through the generic (`source_type`, `source_id`), and it is not in the query. It becomes DATABLE with a per-source-type resolver that fails closed for unknown types.
2. **AP_PREPAYMENT_GL_SUBLEDGER.** This is an account-level GL-vs-current-subledger reconciliation, like #26, which suggests SYSTEMIC. But each advance account is dedicated, so an anchor exists: the earliest cutover or journal date on the account. In practice that anchor is the cutover, so either class blocks every engine date.

## 6. Notes for the engine

- **Confirmation should be per key.** `confirmed` is currently `jsonb_array_length(v_fg)>0` or `(v_material)>0`, which is global. For a DATED_EQUIVALENT check, every key failing the current-state check should appear in the detector output. That is guaranteed for NEGATIVE_FG_BALANCE. For NEGATIVE_MATERIAL_LOCATION_BALANCE it holds only under the writer invariant described in #2, which is why that check is DATABLE here and not DATED_EQUIVALENT.
- **Cache on a dated document (policy-sensitive).** These are classified DATABLE because the cached field belongs to a dated document or entity, and it cannot matter before that entity exists: #12, #28 (status part), #32, #84, #91 (lot-cache part), #97 (status part), and the `po_hpp_gl_state` checks #77, #79, #85. If the auditor rules that every cache mismatch is current state (as with #4), these move to SYSTEMIC.
- **Anchors must fail closed.** A NULL anchor must block every date, and entity anchors must use the **full** fact list. The engine's RECOST fact list (4 kinds plus `recalc_from`) is too narrow to serve as `PO_ANCHOR`.
- **#66 protects the GL detector.** GL_INVENTORY_NEGATIVE_ASOF trusts `account_daily_balances`, so V268_DAILY_BALANCE_LEDGER_MISMATCH should always be evaluated (dated) together with it.
- **Out-of-scope names.** These are never emitted as CRITICAL or ERROR: `STALE_RECOST_QUEUE` and `FAILED_RECOST_QUEUE` (WARN), `V268_COST_RECALC_PENDING` (WARNING), and `V268_BROWSER_DIRECT_FINANCIAL_WRITE` (filtered out by run_v268).
