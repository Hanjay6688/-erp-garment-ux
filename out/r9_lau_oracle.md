## Source section B — Master contract

### Controlling master clauses captured

- **Owner-approved design (M:3722–3739):** per-vendor master; choose a fixed package or priced component checkboxes; physical work, pricing/estimate/agreement, and invoice are distinct; physical flow may be recorded with truly unknown price while HPP/report status stays incomplete; selected package/components must not double count; component quantity follows work actually performed; SKU is optional for WIP lacking final SKU; later invoice is allocated/reconciled to existing work and must not duplicate physical quantity or cost; posted delivery price comes from a snapshot, not today's master. Example amounts are illustrative, not production rates; taxes, batch terms, billing of BS/missing, cash rounding, and book date remain undecided (M:3730–3739).
- **Input/version integrity (M:4139–4143):** master amounts finite and nonnegative; zero only if explicitly configured/free; unit/basis and periods valid; vendor/process/package source must match; no overlapping active versions. Disable while preserving history. Preview and posting use same server resolver; server recomputes amount rather than trusting client total. Changes after preview invalidate/recompute it. A query failure, malformed/overlapping rate, or insufficient permission is an error and must not be converted into intentional unknown-price processing. No unrelated vendor's missing rate globally blocks valid work.
- **Unknown-price physical flow (M:4145–4152):** delivery/receipt facts may post without invoice or fictional price if the authorized unknown path is explicitly chosen. Custody and quantity are conserved exactly, including partial and outstanding quantity. Known/estimated/unknown obligation is explicit and tracked. Unknown components keep related HPP/report financially incomplete; known subtotal is not final total. Do not journal a fabricated zero where the required value is missing.
- **Failed wash/rework/claim (M:4204–4216):** a real paid failed attempt may have service cost without false GOOD/BS, with custody `RETRY_AT_VENDOR` or `RETURN_UNPROCESSED`; an actual new attempt is a new service source, but same UUID HTTP retry is not. Rewash from BS has current vendor fee 0; changing it requires a new effective-dated policy. Claim recovery/credit must preserve physical receipt; do not both recover goods and silently retain full vendor compensation (M:4206–4210). Preserve agreement/pricing date separately from sent, actual-complete, returned, invoice, system, and book dates. Do not reprice a delivered source on receipt date; no restatement policy inferred for open periods; closed period uses controlled adjustment, filed snapshots remain unchanged (M:4212–4216).
- **Invoice correction/reversal (M:4218–4222):** draft invoice is editable per lifecycle; posted invoice correction/credit/reversal is linked and payment dependencies are respected. Cost-only reversal does not move goods. Physical reversal checks QC/FG/sale/claim/cost descendants and refuses or uses official correction; it must not erase history. Late invoice/correction propagates to descendants including sold FG/COGS by an authorized method. Never use current master to inverse a historical transaction.
- **Original 36-case statements (M:4339–4374):** preserve each ID and scenario. These lines are the governing original case descriptions; detailed test oracles below expand them only with contract consequences and mark unresolved policy choices. Test values in these scenarios are synthetic examples, not vendor rates.
- **Decision limits (M:4472–4479):** DEC01 unit basis (PCS/batch/minimum/active rate), DEC02 billed quantity basis (GOOD/BS/missing/failed/component/attempt), DEC03 agreement date/extras/discount/rounding/tax, DEC04 unknown-price sales/close authority, DEC05 SKU override scope/fallback, and DEC06 service/accrual/variance map/post-payment correction remain unresolved. Known baseline: master per vendor; package or components; process known even if price unknown; invoice pending differs from price unknown; SKU optional; no quantity/cost duplication; posted snapshots immutable.
- **No scope shortcut (M:1753–1757):** LAU-DEC01–06 can be prototyped with clearly synthetic fixtures, but actual rates, billing basis, accounts/variance, and unknown-price sales/close permission remain held until owner configures them. Pending does not accept a flow that must be final. If CR enters CP6, its relevant acceptance must be complete or owner must explicitly revise scope.

### Contract consequences used throughout the oracles

1. Delivery post fixes exact source identity, vendor, process, requested quantity, delivery date/time, applicable rate/version or explicit UNKNOWN, estimate/obligation status, and an immutable pricing snapshot. Never derive a replacement rate from receipt date or current master.
2. Receipt/QC posts only actual returned physical quantity and actual process/custody, tied to original delivery. Preserve outstanding, returned, GOOD, BS, missing and failed-attempt counts as separate measures. Enforce capacity/conservation atomically; source's remaining balance is never negative.
3. Physical posting itself does not create FG; final SKU/QC allocation is a separate authorized event and must conserve counts and preserve product/SKU identity. No extra/double FG or cost is introduced by invoice matching.
4. Cost postings and invoice allocation require valid vendor/source lineage, supported configured billing basis, active policy/account mapping, and exact amount reconciliation. If the basis, rate, or mapping needed for a final financial action is unknown, keep that action pending/refuse. Never substitute zero.
5. A committed action retried with same identity must return/recover the prior result without duplicate quantity, service attempt, invoice allocation, accrual, or journal. A genuinely new failed-wash attempt requires its own event identity/source. Reverse posted items only via linked contract-supported inverse/correction, respecting dependent work and closed-period policy.

## Per-case oracle matrix

### Shared execution contract for all 36

- Every case requires an isolated, disposable fixture with named vendor(s), product/source lineage, location, actor/role, business timezone, as-of date, effective pricing version(s), and before-state snapshot. Use synthetic example rates only where the M case provides them (LAU-T02–T05); otherwise use fixture values clearly labeled synthetic and never present them as real policy.
- Positive cases are ACCEPT only for a fully authorized, valid, fresh request. Negative/malformed/unknown-policy cases REFUSE or remain explicitly PENDING as stated below. Rejection is atomic: no partial stock/custody, vendor charge, accrual, AP, FG, claim, or journal mutation. Every accepted effect is exact and linked to source, vendor, process, actor, request/idempotency key, date, and immutable version/snapshot.
- Delivery/receipt require the existing laundry-operation authority for that location. Master/rate writes require owner/admin-configured master authority; invoice draft/post/correction/payment each require distinct configured authority. LAU-DEC06 and LAU-T33 do not authorize any new role. If a policy role or mapping is not configured, REFUSE/PENDING. Logs and UI must not leak financial details to roles lacking access.
- Same committed request identity is idempotent (recover prior result, no second effect). New actual work requires a new source/event identity and cannot exceed source capacity. Posted financial/physical records are immutable; use linked inverse/correction, with payment and downstream dependency guards. No blanket reversal of posted invoice or physical receipt.
- “Exact value” below means the indicated formula/source amount in the contract, carried in exact monetary precision at the supported minor unit and reconciled at each allocation/journal. Where required account/date/billing policy is open, record permissible physical state and keep financial/final report state pending; do not create a zero journal, guessed account or fabricated rate.
